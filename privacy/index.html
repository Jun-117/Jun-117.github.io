Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

KORA CARE Private Limited (Pokhara-13, Kaski, Nepal) provides a water-purifier subscription for homes in Pokhara.

What we collect

When you message us on Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp or email, we receive your name or handle as shown by that service, your account ID on that service, your messages, the files you send, and the time they were sent. If you are a customer, we also keep the details you give us for installation, service and billing.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you and to provide our service: installation, filter changes, billing and support. We do not sell your data and we do not use your messages for advertising.

Where it is stored and who sees it

On Google Cloud (Firebase) servers in Mumbai, India. Only KORA CARE staff who handle customer support can see it. Meta, Google, Zoho and YCloud (our WhatsApp provider) also keep copies of messages sent through their services, under their own policies.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to serve you and as Nepali law requires for billing records. Spam and messages unrelated to our service are deleted within 30 days.

Your choices / delete your data

Email contact@koracarenepal.com or WhatsApp +977 971-4612210 and ask us to show, correct or delete your messages. We will do it within 30 days, except billing records we must keep by law.
